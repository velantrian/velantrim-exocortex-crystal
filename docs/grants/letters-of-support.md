# Letters of Support — outreach kit

A few short letters of support from European open-source, research or
civic-technology actors materially strengthen the **European Dimension** of an
NLnet NGI0 Commons Fund application. They are not a formal requirement, but they
signal that the project is wanted by the community it serves.

This kit is a practical template. Nothing here is sent automatically — it is
material for the maintainer to adapt and send personally.

## What a strong supporter looks like

Aim for 2–4 short endorsements from people or organisations that are:

- **European** (or have a clear European footprint) — reinforces the EU angle;
- **relevant**: FOSS maintainers, digital-rights / civic-tech groups, research
  groups, libraries/archives, universities, or developers building on local-first
  / privacy-preserving / knowledge-graph technology;
- **credible and independent**: not the applicant; a genuine third-party voice.

Quality over quantity. One specific, honest paragraph from a real maintainer is
worth more than a generic letterhead.

## What to ask them to say

A useful letter is short (5–10 sentences) and concrete. It should touch:

1. **Who they are** and why their view is relevant (one line).
2. **The problem they recognise** — opaque, unverifiable AI memory; lock-in to
   cloud-only providers; lack of provenance/auditability.
3. **Why Velantrim's approach matters to them** — local-first, source-linked,
   inspectable memory; LLMs as replaceable interfaces, not the source of truth.
4. **A concrete interest**: they would evaluate it, reuse a component, integrate
   it, contribute, or follow the work.
5. **The public-interest / European angle** in their own words.

Ask them to avoid overclaiming on the project's behalf ("eliminates
hallucinations", "production-ready") — honest, specific support is more
persuasive and matches the project's own honesty rule.

## Outreach email (template)

> **Subject:** Quick request: short letter of support for an NLnet open-source application
>
> Hi {Name},
>
> I'm {Your name}, the maintainer of **Velantrim Crystal** — an open-source
> (AGPL-3.0), local-first, *verifiable* AI memory layer. Instead of trusting a
> language model to remember, it stores facts in a canonical graph with
> provenance, epistemic state and source metadata, and every answer comes with a
> replayable receipt. It runs locally by default — no cloud, no telemetry.
> Repository: {repo URL}
>
> I've applied to the **NLnet NGI0 Commons Fund** to turn it from a tested
> research-grade core into a reproducible, deployable open-source MVP. A few
> short letters of support help show the European public-interest community wants
> this kind of infrastructure.
>
> Would you be willing to write a short paragraph (5–10 sentences) on why
> transparent, local-first, source-grounded AI memory matters to you or your
> work, and any interest you'd have in evaluating, reusing or following it? I've
> attached a one-page template you can adapt — honest and specific is perfect; no
> need to overstate anything.
>
> No pressure and no rush — even a couple of lines by {date} would help a lot.
> Thank you for considering it.
>
> Best,
> {Your name}
> {contact / repo / optional links}

## Where to find supporters (starting points)

- FOSS maintainers in adjacent areas: knowledge graphs, local-first software,
  PKM/note tools, privacy-preserving infrastructure, self-hosting.
- Communities already engaged: prior public interest (e.g. discussion threads,
  Reddit/forum conversations) is a warm starting list — reach out to people who
  already engaged with the idea.
- European digital-rights / civic-tech organisations and research groups.
- NLnet's own ecosystem: maintainers of currently/previously funded projects in
  related domains (see https://nlnet.nl/project/current.html) may relate to the
  goals.

## Handling and privacy

- Keep supporters' personal contact details **out of the public repository**.
  Collect letters privately and submit them through the channel NLnet indicates.
- Only publish a letter publicly with the supporter's explicit consent, and
  prefer name + role + organisation over personal contact details.
- Track outreach privately (a simple local list); do not commit it.

## Status

This is outreach material, not a claim of received endorsements. Any letters
obtained are submitted to NLnet directly; they are not part of the codebase.
