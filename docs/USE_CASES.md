# Use Cases

Velantrim Crystal is a local-first, verifiable AI memory core. It is not a chatbot UI. It is the memory layer that can sit under agents, assistants, local apps and institutional tools.

## Private users

A user can keep long-term project memory, personal notes, preferences and decisions in a local store. The goal is continuity without mandatory cloud memory.

## Schools and universities

An institution can keep curriculum facts, definitions, reading lists, lessons and internal procedures in a local, source-tracked knowledge base.

## Research

Researchers can store claims, notes, source references, contradictions and evolving interpretations with provenance and replayable receipts.

## Public sector

A public organisation can keep internal documentation, policies and procedures in a local memory system with audit trails and controlled retrieval.

## Business

A company can store support playbooks, product facts, internal policies and decision records without turning unsupported claims into operational memory.

## Field operations

Agriculture, industry and technical teams can store procedures, safety notes, maintenance records and field observations for offline-capable retrieval.

## Libraries and archives

Curated corpora can be turned into source-tracked claims, making search and AI-assisted retrieval more auditable than plain chunk search.

## Boundary

Domain apps, dashboards and browser demos should stay separate from the Crystal core unless they use the same TruthGate, provenance and receipt boundary.
